## Decision note (documented deviation, research A5)

CONTEXT's locked bullet says the embed moves to "@12"; this plan pins `mermaid@12.0.0` instead. Rationale: a floating @12 tag re-resolves when 12.0.1 publishes (7-day jsDelivr cache) and silently invalidates the SRI hash in every already-copied embed — defeating the same locked decision's demand for a *valid* hash, and contradicting the milestone's locked exact-pin philosophy. RESEARCH explicitly recommends the exact pin (Open Question 1 / assumption A5). This note is the visible record of that one-word deviation.
