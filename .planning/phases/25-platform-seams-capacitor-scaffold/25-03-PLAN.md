<objective>
Make AI absent — not hidden — from the native build (PLAT-05): gate all six AI entry surfaces behind `supportsAI()` from the platform seam, with native-flag tests proving each surface is unreachable while the web behavior stays byte-for-byte behavioral-identical.

Purpose: "No AI in v1" is a product constraint (CONTEXT/ROADMAP scope boundary): WebGPU inference is unrealistic in an Android WebView, and shipping dead AI entry points in the APK is both a UX lie (taps that go nowhere) and payload weight. The chunk-level stripping already landed in 25-01; this plan removes the reachable paths so the stripped chunks have no live callers.

Output: six gated surfaces, extended native-flag tests, and build-level proof that the native payload carries no AI runtime bytes.
</objective>
